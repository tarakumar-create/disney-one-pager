Disney's digital footprint spans ABC, ESPN, Marvel, National Geographic, and parks and resorts experiences. Each property carries its own analytics stack, event taxonomy, and measurement conventions, which makes a single trusted view of guest behavior hard to achieve.
Across the business, teams struggle to:
  • Validate event data across Adobe and other platforms before broken schemas and taxonomies reach downstream teams.
  • Unify guest journeys across streaming, sports, news, and parks properties that each track behavior differently.
  • Enforce privacy and consent at scale, guaranteeing that personal information never leaks into analytics pipelines.
  • Turn insight into action instead of static reporting, so behavioral signals actually drive experiments and personalization.
